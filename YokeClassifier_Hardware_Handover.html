§ 1 Document Purpose

This document is intended for the hardware setup team responsible for the physical installation, connection, and commissioning of the YokeClassifier inspection station. It provides all guidance required to procure, install, connect, and verify the hardware needed to bring the inspection station to operational readiness.

This document covers:

  • Hardware Bill of Materials (BOM) and procurement reference
  • Physical installation of all hardware components
  • Camera mounting, alignment, and positional requirements
  • Fixture/jig installation and placement
  • LED lighting installation and configuration
  • Display installation and connection
  • Buzzer installation and connection
  • Wiring, power, and cable management
  • Hardware-level verification checklist
  • Final handover procedure and sign-off
Important: The YokeClassifier software and trained machine-learning inspection model are fully complete and operational. The project/software team will connect the operational software to the installed hardware during final system verification. No software modification, rewriting, or retraining is required at any stage of this handover.
§ 2 System Overview

YokeClassifier is a real-time automated visual inspection system for manufactured automotive yoke components. The completed system performs the following operational sequence:

OPERATOR places yoke component
↓
Consistent positioning
FIXTURE / JIG holds component in fixed orientation
↓
Image acquisition
CAMERA captures component image
↓
USB video stream to Mini PC
MINI PC — YokeClassifier processes image
↓
Automated inspection result
Inspection regions identified & highlighted on display
↓
Result output
DISPLAY: PASS ✓
DISPLAY: FAIL ✗ + BUZZER

All software logic, inspection algorithms, and model parameters are pre-configured. Hardware installation does not require any software changes.

§ 4 Hardware Installation Responsibilities
4.1 Hardware Setup Team

The hardware setup team is solely responsible for:

  1. Providing / procuring all hardware listed in the Bill of Materials (§ 3).
  2. Installing the Mini PC in a secure, ventilated location.
  3. Mounting the camera on the adjustable stand at the correct position above the fixture.
  4. Installing and securing the inspection fixture / jig on the workstation surface.
  5. Installing the LED ring light to provide uniform illumination of the inspection area.
  6. Installing and connecting the display to the Mini PC via HDMI.
  7. Connecting the USB buzzer to the Mini PC.
  8. Connecting all power and data cables between components.
  9. Securing and routing all cables to prevent obstruction and trip hazards.
  10. Ensuring the physical stability of the entire inspection station.
  11. Completing the hardware-level verification checklist (§ 9).
  12. Coordinating with the project/software team for final functional verification.
4.2 Project / Software Team

After hardware installation is complete, the project/software team will:

  1. Connect the completed YokeClassifier software to the installed hardware.
  2. Verify that the camera feed is correctly received by the system.
  3. Verify the inspection interface displays correctly on the connected display.
  4. Verify PASS/FAIL detection with known test components.
  5. Verify buzzer response on a FAIL detection event.
  6. Perform final system-level validation and sign-off.
Clarification: The project/software team is not required to rewrite, retrain, modify, or debug the YokeClassifier software. The software is complete. Their role in this handover is limited to final integration verification with the installed hardware.

§ 5 Physical Installation Requirements
5.1 Compute Unit (Mini PC)
  • Install the Mini PC securely on the workstation or in a designated enclosure.
  • Ensure adequate ventilation clearance on all sides; do not enclose without airflow.
  • Protect the unit from dust, vibration, and risk of accidental impact.
  • Provide a stable, regulated power supply.
  • Position it such that all required cables can be routed cleanly without strain on connectors.
5.2 Camera
  • Mount the camera securely on the adjustable stand; lock all adjustable joints firmly after positioning.
  • Point the camera lens directly toward the inspection fixture from above.
  • Ensure the entire required inspection region of the yoke component is visible within the camera frame.
  • Verify there is no vibration transmitted to the camera mount from nearby machinery.
  • Avoid unnecessary movement of the camera after final positioning.
⚠ Camera Position Warning
  • The camera position, height, angle, and distance are calibrated to the validated inspection setup.
  • Do not change the camera position after final verification without consulting the project/software team.
  • Any change in camera geometry may require full re-validation of the inspection system.
5.3 Fixture / Jig

The inspection fixture is critical for consistent, repeatable results. The fixture must:

  • Hold the yoke component in the same orientation for every inspection cycle.
  • Prevent any unwanted movement of the component during inspection.
  • Maintain a consistent orientation relative to the camera's field of view.
  • Allow the operator to load and unload components easily and quickly.
  • Keep the component entirely within the camera's intended inspection area.

Notify the project/software team before any fixture modification.

5.4 LED Ring Light

Consistent, uniform illumination is essential for reliable inspection results.

  • Mount the LED ring light around or adjacent to the camera lens, directed at the inspection area.
  • Position the light to provide uniform, even illumination across the entire component surface.

Avoid the following conditions:

  • Harsh shadows caused by indirect or angled light sources.
  • Glare or hot-spots on the component surface.
  • Strong reflections from metallic yoke surfaces.
  • Uneven illumination between different regions of the component.

Once the lighting position is validated, it must remain fixed.

5.5 Display
  • Mount or place the display so the operator can clearly see it while loading/unloading components.
  • Connect via HDMI to the Mini PC.
  • The display must clearly present: the live camera feed, highlighted inspection regions, PASS status, FAIL status, and any other information shown by YokeClassifier.
5.6 USB Buzzer
  • Connect the USB buzzer to an available USB port on the Mini PC.
  • Position the buzzer so the alert tone is clearly audible to the operator at the working position.
  • The buzzer is activated automatically by YokeClassifier upon a FAIL detection event.

§ 8 Step-by-Step Installation Procedure

Follow the steps below in sequence. Do not skip steps or change the order without prior consultation.

1

Install the Inspection Fixture / Jig

Place the custom inspection fixture on the workstation surface at the designated inspection position. Secure it firmly so it does not shift during operation. Verify that the component loading area is accessible.

2

Install the Camera Mount / Stand

Position the adjustable camera stand such that its arm is centered above the inspection fixture. Ensure the stand base is stable and cannot tip. Do not tighten arm joints fully at this stage.

3

Mount the Camera

Attach the ELP USB camera to the mount. Orient the lens directly downward toward the fixture. Adjust height so the component will be fully visible. Final tightening occurs in Step 5.

4

Install the LED Ring Light

Attach the LED ring light around or adjacent to the camera lens so that light is directed at the inspection fixture. Connect the ring light power supply to the power strip.

5

Align Camera and Lighting

Place a sample yoke component in the fixture. Verify that the entire inspection area is visible in the camera frame. Adjust camera height, angle, and ring light position to achieve uniform, glare-free illumination. Tighten and lock all mount joints firmly. Record the final camera height and position for reference.

6

Install the Mini PC

Place the Intel N100 Mini PC in a secure, ventilated location on or adjacent to the workstation. Ensure adequate clearance around all ventilation openings. Connect the power adapter to the Mini PC and to the power strip.

7

Install the Display

Place or mount the 7-inch HDMI display at a position clearly visible to the operator. Connect the HDMI cable from the display to the Mini PC's HDMI port. Connect the display power supply to the power strip.

8

Connect the Buzzer

Plug the USB buzzer into an available USB port on the Mini PC. Position the buzzer so its alert tone is clearly audible to the operator from the standard working position.

9

Connect the Camera

Connect the ELP USB camera to an available USB port on the Mini PC. Ensure the cable has sufficient slack to allow minor positional adjustment without stressing the connector.

10

Secure and Route All Cables

Route all cables (USB, HDMI, power) along the workstation frame or through cable management channels. Fasten cables using cable ties or clips. Ensure no cables cross the operator's working area or create trip hazards.

11

Power On and Hardware Verification

Switch on the power strip. Power on the Mini PC, display, and ring light. Complete the Hardware Verification Checklist (§ 9). Do not launch YokeClassifier software — that is the responsibility of the project/software team.

12

Coordinate Final System Verification

Notify the project/software team that hardware installation is complete and the verification checklist has been signed off. The project/software team will then perform final functional verification of the YokeClassifier system on the installed hardware.

§ 10 Final Functional Verification

Final functional verification is the responsibility of the project/software team and must be performed after the hardware setup team has completed and signed off the checklist above.

PASS Test — Known-Good Component
Input Condition

Place a known-good (non-defective) yoke component in the inspection fixture.

Expected Result
  • Component is correctly detected within the inspection frame.
  • Valid inspection regions are identified and displayed/highlighted.
  • PASS result is shown on the display.
  • Buzzer does not activate.
FAIL Test — Known-Defective Component
Input Condition

Place a known-defective yoke component (with a known defect or missing region) in the inspection fixture.

Expected Result
  • Defective or missing region is correctly identified.
  • Defective region is highlighted on the inspection display.
  • FAIL result is shown on the display.
  • Buzzer activates to alert the operator.

Both test cases must produce the expected results before the system is accepted and handover sign-off is completed.

§ 11 Important Do-Not-Change Instructions

The following restrictions protect the validated inspection setup. Any deviation from the installed and verified configuration may invalidate inspection results and requires prior authorization from the project/software team.

⚠ Strictly Do Not Perform the Following
  • Move the camera after final setup and validation.
  • Change the camera height without authorization.
  • Change the camera angle without authorization.
  • Move the LED ring light after validation.
  • Modify fixture dimensions without consulting the project/software team.
  • Replace the camera with a different model or type without verification.
  • Disconnect hardware (camera, buzzer, display) while the system is operating.
  • Modify the YokeClassifier software in any way.
  • Replace, delete, or rename model files without authorization from the project/software team.
  • Change system configuration settings without consulting the project/software team.
The YokeClassifier system is calibrated to a specific camera geometry and lighting condition. Any uncoordinated physical change may require full re-validation of the inspection system from the beginning.
§ 12 Maintenance Guidelines

The following routine maintenance activities are recommended to keep the inspection station operating reliably. No specialized technical skills are required.

# Maintenance Activity Recommended Frequency Notes
1 Clean the camera lens Weekly or as required Use a clean, dry microfiber cloth. Avoid solvents.
2 Clean the LED ring light surface Weekly or as required Remove dust and oil deposits that may affect illumination uniformity.
3 Check all cable connections Monthly Verify USB and HDMI connectors are fully seated and undamaged.
4 Verify fixture mechanical stability Monthly Check that mounting fasteners are tight and the fixture has not shifted.
5 Check camera position has not shifted Monthly Verify the camera mount joints are still locked and position is unchanged.
6 Check lighting position has not shifted Monthly Verify ring light alignment relative to camera and fixture.
7 Ensure Mini PC ventilation is clear Monthly Remove dust accumulation near ventilation openings.
8 Keep inspection area clean Daily Remove metal shavings, oil, and debris from fixture and surrounding area.

If any hardware component fails or shows degradation, contact the project/software team before attempting replacement to ensure compatibility with the validated inspection setup.
